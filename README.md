## RAGE QUIT //

A small desktop-first, turn-based roguelike deckbuilder set in a fictional chatroom. Play cards to raise each opponent's tilt, manage your focus through their replies, and draft a new card after each cleared room. Clear all three rooms to win.

### Play

Open `index.html` in a browser (or serve this folder with any static web server). Click cards to play them, then choose **End Turn** to see the opponent's reply and draw a fresh hand. The **i** button explains the rules; **View Deck** shows your current loadout. Runs are randomized and restartable.
